RepX App Prototype
REPX · WORKING PROTOTYPE

An AI strength coach in your pocket

Tap through the app on the right. Everything responds, and the plan you build carries across every screen.

  1. Train: press Start. The AI recognizes the exercise, counts reps and flags form. Switch on Add mistakes to see corrections and partial reps.
  2. Coach: ask the AI to change your plan, e.g. “My knee hurts on squats”. It edits the real plan.
  3. Profile: change sport, equipment or injuries, then rebuild the program.
  4. Today: connect a wearable and toggle a bad night’s sleep to watch the session adapt.

The camera view uses a simulated athlete because this page can’t open your camera. The rep counting, form rules, program builder and chat coach run for real.
